# AI Reflection

## How I Used AI

I used ChatGPT to help plan and write the two C++ programs for this lab. It helped me understand how to calculate the average of five numbers and how to project ocean-level rise over different periods.

## Decisions I Made

For `average.cpp`, I used variables to store the five numbers and calculated their sum and average. For `ocean_levels.cpp`, I used a constant for the annual ocean-level rise and calculated the rise after five, seven, and ten years.

## Verification

I tested both programs using the assigned values and alternate test values. The average program returned a sum of 154 and an average of 30.8 for the assigned values. With alternate values, it returned a sum of 61 and an average of 12.2. The ocean-level program returned 7.5, 10.5, and 15 millimeters with the assigned rate. With an alternate rate of 2.3 millimeters per year, it returned 11.5, 16.1, and 23 millimeters. The results matched the expected calculations.

## What I Learned

I learned how variables, constants, arithmetic operations, and output statements work together in C++. Testing with different values helped me verify that the programs calculated the correct results.

I will continue practicing writing C++ programs independently, choosing appropriate variable types, and testing programs with different values.
